# Migzu's HandyPhone

**Migzu's HandyPhone is a Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3 that adds a working smartphone to multiplayer servers:** texting, group chats,
photos, maps with directions and voice calls (as a Simple Voice Chat addon). Press **U** and the phone comes out in your hand, no item needed.

**Download:** [modrinth.com/mod/handyphone](https://modrinth.com/mod/handyphone). **Bugs and ideas:** open an [issue](../../issues).

## Features
- **Messages and groups:** every player gets a phone number; text anyone on the server, create group chats, save contacts, get notifications,
  and receive messages sent while you were offline.
- **Camera and gallery:** take photos without your hand or the HUD, keep them in a gallery and send them in any chat; drag an image from your
  computer or paste it with Ctrl+V.
- **Map and directions:** see where you are, send your location or a dropped pin, share your live location (15 min, 1 h or 8 h), save places,
  and tap "Go here" to follow an arrow and a beam of light. With squaremap on the server, the map shows the real terrain.
- **Voice calls:** with Simple Voice Chat, call any player or a whole group, from anywhere and across dimensions.
- **Four styles:** Classic (2009), Aero (2012), Minimal (2016) and Modern, each with its own sounds.
- **Six languages:** English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German and Russian.
- **For server owners:** the server stores everything, moderation commands (`/handyphone mute`, `unmute`, `info`, `wipe`), anti-spam limits,
  blocking, and a config file to turn features on or off. Messages are not encrypted: the server owner can read them.

## Requirements
Minecraft 26.3, Fabric Loader and Fabric API, on **both client and server**. Optional: Simple Voice Chat (calls) and squaremap (map terrain).
Works with Sodium and Iris.
